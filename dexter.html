DARK·PASSENGER

A Dexter Fan Case — Miami, Tonight
You are Dexter Morgan. Blood-spatter analyst by day. Something else after dark. A body turns up on the boardwalk and Debra needs your eyes. Follow the blood, work the evidence, and decide — by the Code — whether tonight ends on your table.
Unofficial, non-commercial fan project inspired by the TV series “Dexter”. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the rights holders. No copyrighted assets or music are used — all visuals and audio are generated placeholders. Runs best on a desktop browser with a mouse.

HOW TO PLAY

Move — W A S D  ·  Run — Shift  ·  Jump — Space

Look — move the mouse (click the game to capture the cursor)

Interact — walk up to a glowing marker and press E to examine evidence or talk to people

Notebook — Tab opens your case file (evidence, suspect, the Code, objectives)

Pause / Save — Esc

Follow the beam of light — it always points to your current objective. Collect enough evidence to identify the suspect, confirm all three points of the Code, then finish the case.
